### 🔭 Building
 
**🔐 [q-SAST](https://github.com/Angeloth1/q_Sast_mvp)** — static analysis for Python that finds quantum-vulnerable cryptography and estimates what breaking it would cost a fault-tolerant quantum computer, citing the exact table or equation behind every number. Designed as a contribution platform: one paper → one model → one file.  
*Current coverage: RSA key generation, Beauregard (2003) model.*
 
**🏋️ Get Shredded** — offline-first workout tracker: sets and timers are saved locally and synced to the backend in the background once the connection returns.  
*React Native (Expo) · Zustand · Supabase*
 
### 📄 Research
 
**[Evaluation of Variational Quantum Classifiers (VQC) for Cyberattack Detection in the NISQ Era](https://www.mdpi.com/2224-2708/15/4/67)**  
Angelos Thomos, Theodore Andronikos · *Journal of Sensor and Actuator Networks* 15(4), 67 (2026) · open access
 
A 4-qubit VQC for network intrusion detection on NSL-KDD, benchmarked against classical baselines across multiple seeds, reporting where the quantum model falls short as well as where it holds up. [Code & full reproduction →](https://github.com/Angeloth1/Quantum-IDS-NSLKDD/tree/revisionMDPI)
 
Follow-up paper on angle encoding in progress.
